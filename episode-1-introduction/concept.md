# Episode 1 — Introduction

The object is a brass and dark enamel grand orrery on a turned, walnut instrument base. Its mode selector replaces Earth-centered epicycle machinery with Sun-centered orbit carriers, then changes the circular rails to ellipses. Mars is the witness planet: its motion and live readout show what each change explains.

## Mechanism and scope

- Ptolemaic: a stationary Earth, schematic classical body carriers and an elevated Mars deferent/epicycle mechanism. Mars uses the two circular frequencies of a planet/Earth decomposition and exhibits retrograde motion. Eccentrics, equant and inclination are omitted.
- Copernican: six planets revolve around a stationary Sun on idealized circles. Retrograde motion arises from relative Earth/Mars motion. This simplified reconstruction omits the additional circles retained by historical Copernicus.
- Newtonian: solve Kepler's equation for each ellipse, with the Sun at a focus. Mars's eccentricity is adjustable, initially 0.094. Instantaneous radius, speed and solar acceleration follow that same solution. The model is a fixed-Sun two-body approximation, not a modern ephemeris.
- Orbital distances are compressed, globe sizes enlarged and planetary inclinations omitted. The mechanical carrier heights separate the mechanisms. The display is an explanatory instrument, not a scale replica of a surviving orrery.

## Parts list

Base: four leveling pads, four turned feet, four foot collars, walnut plinth, lower brass trim, upper brass trim, four mounting washers and screws.

Drum: eight support columns, sixteen column collars, lower bearing disk, toothed drive wheel, upper brass drum, two drum seams, enamel dial face, outer bezel, two concentric engraved rim lines, 120 rim graduation marks and twelve bezel fasteners.

Central structure: mounting cup, nested carrier hubs, central spindle, lower collar, knurled crown, central Earth or Sun globe, engraved instrument plaque and two plaque rivets.

Planet carriers: radial arms, terminal shoes, globe stems and globes, with Saturn's open ring and Jupiter's equatorial band. Moon and Sun carriers join the geocentric configuration.

Ptolemaic module: raised Mars support, deferent arm, epicycle bearing, open epicycle ring, three support spokes, epicycle arm, terminal shoe, Mars stem and globe.

Controls: three historical mode selectors, play/pause, playback speed, time scrubber, reset, theme switch, planet selection and Newtonian eccentricity adjustment.
